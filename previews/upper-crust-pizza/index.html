Dough made fresh daily

Hi-gluten unbleached wheat flour, mixed and hand-tossed every morning. Whole wheat crust is available too, and the vegetables are cut fresh each day.

Our own mozzarella blend

We have blended our own lower-fat mozzarella for over 30 years, and the sauce starts with minimally processed, fresh-packed tomatoes.

Room for the whole crew

A remodeled, spacious dining room with big screens for the game, beer and wine, and a small game room in the back for the kids.

Pizza, calzones, pasta and more

Specialty pizzas come in 8, 10, 12 and 14 inch, or create your own. Ask about our 10 inch gluten-free or cauliflower crust. Lunch specials with salad and a drink run Tuesday to Friday, 11 to 3, and weeknight dinner specials rotate Tuesday through Thursday.

A local favorite since 1992

Upper Crust Pizza has been rolling it right at the corner of College Blvd and Oceanside Blvd since 1992. Owners Tim and Danielle Falvey are a husband-and-wife team dedicated to good food and great service, and you can always ask for either of them when you come in.

The idea is simple: quality over quantity, and people are everything. We focus on pizza and the things that go with it instead of trying to do everything, and we make our dough fresh every day.

Regulars know the ranch, the gluten-free Everything pizza and the game room in the back. New neighbors are always welcome. Eat local, eat healthy.

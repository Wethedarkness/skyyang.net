Our story

A local favorite since 1992

Upper Crust Pizza has been rolling it right at the corner of College Blvd and Oceanside Blvd since 1992. Owners Tim and Danielle Falvey are a husband-and-wife team dedicated to good food and great service, and you can always ask for either of them when you come in.

The idea is simple: quality over quantity, and people are everything. We focus on pizza and the things that go with it instead of trying to do everything, and we make our dough fresh every day.

Regulars know the ranch, the gluten-free Everything pizza and the game room in the back. New neighbors are always welcome. Eat local, eat healthy.

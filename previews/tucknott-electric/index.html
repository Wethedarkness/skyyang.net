A Tri-Valley electrical contractor since 1972

Tucknott Electric Company was established in 1972 by Robert A. “Bob” Tucknott, a licensed electrical contractor, Certified Professional Estimator and graduate of a four-year electrical apprenticeship. More than five decades later the company still runs from its Livermore office with the same promise: the buck stops here.

We have built a solid reputation among major industrial and commercial accounts and many residential clients for looking at a project with a critical and objective attitude, one that often results in substantial savings of time, money and energy. Our project managers have no allegiances to any manufacturer's equipment, so we are free to design in the best performance at the best cost.

Tucknott Electric has been recognized by the Associated Builders and Contractors as an Accredited Quality Contractor for multiple consecutive years, along with ABC's Platinum STEP safety award and National Safety Merit Awards from 2008 through 2011. We hold memberships with ABC, the American Society of Professional Estimators, the Association of Professional Energy Managers and the Forensic Expert Witness Association.

Expert witness and consulting

Through Robert A. Tucknott and Associates, Inc., Bob serves as a certified forensic electrical expert witness and consultant with over 40 years of experience in the electrical construction and maintenance industry. He has extensive experience in fire and impact damage cases, shock and electrocution claims, construction-defect matters and coverage determinations, and has been called in by multiple insurance companies to evaluate claims.

Bob is a Certified Forensic Litigation Consultant (CFLC) and a board member of the Forensic Expert Witness Association, an arbitrator for the Construction Division of the American Arbitration Association, and a Senior Arbitrator on the Better Business Bureau's National Panel of Consumer Arbitrators. Most cases settle before trial; he has extensive deposition and trial experience.

Forensic work runs through Forensic Electrical Experts.

  • Shock and electrocution injury claims
  • Electrical and construction defect matters

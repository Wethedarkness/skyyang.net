Built on faith, family, and a love for bringing people together through food

Cherie Barfield grew up in Dublin in a family where cooking was how you showed love: a father raised in a small Louisiana village, a mother with Mississippi and St. Louis roots, and summers in Beaumont, Texas learning to make a proper gumbo roux.

After a 28-year corporate career, it was her son Bryce's class business plan that convinced her to take the leap. Cherie's Southern Kitchen opened on Father's Day 2023 as the first Black-owned, woman-owned soul food restaurant in Dublin's history.

The Cherie's experience is meant to feel like a home gathering, where you can feel the love radiating from the dishes. Cherie still makes the desserts herself.

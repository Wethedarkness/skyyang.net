How an order goes

From your list of names to finished pieces, the same four steps whether it is one desk plate or badges for a whole department.

  1. 1

    Send the details

    Names, titles, quantity and your logo. Vector files help; a photo of an existing badge works too.

  2. 2

    Approve a proof

    We quote the job and send a layout to check spelling and sizing before anything is engraved.

  3. 3

    We make it

    Rotary, laser, digital print or sublimation, whichever suits the material. Most established jobs turn around in 24 to 48 hours.

  4. 4

    Pick up or ship

    Collect on South B Street, or have it sent. We have customers throughout the country.

Three families, one shop, since 1979

Originally operated as The Badgeman. After a few years, the original owners joined a franchise and became Recognition Express. In 2011 we parted ways from the franchise and renamed the business Spotlight Impressions.

Established in 1979. We are an independently owned and operated business in the San Francisco Bay Area. Although we are physically located in San Mateo, we have many customers throughout the country.

The shop has passed from one husband-and-wife team to a second, and is run today by their daughter and son-in-law. The words above are the shop's own, from its About page and its Yelp history.

Questions people ask first

Answers come from the shop's own site and listings. Anything not covered here goes in the quote form.

How fast can you turn a job around?

Most established jobs turn around in 24 to 48 hours. Say when you need it in the quote request and we will confirm.

What if a name is misspelled?

If any product is misspelled at our doing, we gladly replace it with the correct spelling. You also approve a proof before we engrave.

Which process should I pick?

We work in rotary engraving, laser engraving, digital printing and sublimation. Tell us the material and how the piece will be used, and we will recommend one.

Do you only serve San Mateo?

The shop is at 147 South B Street in downtown San Mateo, and it has customers throughout the country.

How can I pay?

Credit cards, Apple Pay and Android Pay are accepted, as listed on Yelp.

Send us the names. We take it from there.

One badge or two hundred, a desk plate or a wall of plaques. Send the details and you will hear back with a price and a proof.

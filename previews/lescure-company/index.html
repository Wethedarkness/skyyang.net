About us

Three generations of Lescures, since 1947

Lescure Company started in 1947 when Marcel and Charlotte Lescure bought a small Lafayette plumbing shop. Their son Norman rebuilt the business from the late 1950s onward, took it into hospital work and design-build commercial plumbing, and incorporated it in 1966.

In 1998 the third generation took over. Today Mike Lescure serves as president, and the company has grown from a mechanical shop into a general contractor that specializes in jobs where our own crews can perform every scope of work.

Our motto has not changed: think smart, work hard and have integrity. We are a union contractor working with the UA local halls, and most of our people have been with us for a long time.

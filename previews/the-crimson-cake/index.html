A pastry chef's studio, not a factory

The Crimson Cake is owned and operated by Pastry Chef Alicia Phoenix. Alicia graduated from the Scottsdale Culinary Institute in 1999 and spent the next decade honing her baking and cake decorating skills in Boston and North County San Diego, working in wholesale bakeries and upscale hotel pastry shops.

In 2010, after several years of working full time and doing cakes on the side, she launched The Crimson Cake as a full-time cake venture. Today she does most of the cake work herself and relies on a few great ladies who help with all of the baking.

Because every cake is made with fresh ingredients, wedding cakes are kept to 100 servings or fewer. Extra large and high stacks of cake are not worth the risk. What you get instead is a cake that tastes as good as it looks.

Email or call, and we'll send a quote.

Custom orders need at least two weeks' notice. Last-minute requests can sometimes be accommodated if ordered by Monday evening for that week.

  1. 1

    Send the basics

    Email thecrimsoncake@gmail.com with your date, servings, flavors and any inspiration photos.

  2. 2

    Get a quote

    We reply with a quote and an invoice you can pay online.

  3. 3

    Pick up or deliver

    Pick up at the studio, or ask about delivery for larger orders.

  • Walk in: last-minute cakes, cupcakes, macaron and dessert boxes are always stocked
  • Weddings: save the date with no deposit, then finalize your design and flavors
  • Delivery: available with a $200 order minimum plus an address-based fee, after 3:30 pm Wednesday to Friday and by appointment Saturday and Sunday
  • Dietary: most items available gluten-free, many vegan; dairy-free, egg-free, soy-free and nut-free on request (no sugar-free at this time)

A century of Idaho counsel

Davison, Copple, Copple & Copple is a general practice law firm established in 1901. Our philosophy of providing the highest quality legal service possible in a timely, cost-effective and personal manner has remained constant for over a century.

Senior partner E Don Copple has practiced with the firm since 1966 and is admitted before the Idaho courts, the U.S. District Court for the District of Idaho, the Ninth Circuit Court of Appeals and the United States Supreme Court. Senior partner Terry C. Copple has been in private practice in Idaho since 1976.

From our offices on Capitol Boulevard we represent title companies, banks, real estate investors and businesses across Idaho, along with the individuals and families who come to us with a legal problem for the first time.

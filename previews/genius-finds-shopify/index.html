why it's laid out this way
· skyyang333@gmail.com

Homepage: one clear promise above the fold, the primary action (shop best sellers) first, social proof and policies right under it so the first scroll answers "what is this, can I trust it".

Navigation and discovery: a short, flat menu (Shop, Best Sellers, Bundles, Reviews) plus a visible search bar; category tiles immediately below the hero so people self-select in one tap.

Collection cards: rating and review count on every card, honest compare-at pricing, a "save in a bundle" nudge, and an add-to-cart button that does not require opening the product.

Product page: benefits before specs, variants as tap targets, quantity plus add-to-cart in one row, micro-trust under the button, a frequently-bought-together bundle for AOV, and accordions so the page stays short on phones.

Mobile: sticky add-to-cart bar, two-column product grid, thumb-sized buttons, a single sticky header, no carousels that hide content.

Trust: shipping and returns stated in the announcement bar, trust strip and under every buy button, real reviews fed from your review app, payment badges in the footer.

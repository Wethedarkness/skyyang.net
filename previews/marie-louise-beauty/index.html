A family-oriented salon for more than two decades

Marie Louise Beauty Supply and Full Salon is a family-run braiding salon and beauty supply store on Clayton Road in downtown Concord. For more than two decades, Marie Louise has been crafting styles for both men and women, offering braids, cornrows, weaves and extensions.

Her daughter Jeanne joined the business 12 years ago and played a key role in transforming the space. After a recent renovation, the salon now features a bright, open layout and an expanded retail section filled with beauty supplies.

We bring over 20 years of experience in the hair styling business and specialize in styles for men and women. Come in, say hello, and let us take care of you.

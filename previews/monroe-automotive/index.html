About

An independent shop in downtown Chandler

Monroe Automotive and Fleet Specialists is your trusted partner for all your auto repair and car maintenance needs. Owner Jose Aranda opened the business in 2024 and moved it to its current home at 799 S Arizona Ave in downtown Chandler in early 2026, with a grand opening in February.

Customers know Jose by name. Reviews describe a shop that gets you in the same day when it can, explains what needs to be done and what doesn't, and keeps a comfortable lobby for the wait. The shop is BBB Accredited with an A rating and a member of the Chandler Chamber of Commerce.

Whether you drive one car or run a fleet of trucks and buses, the work is backed by the same 36-month, 36,000-mile nationwide warranty.

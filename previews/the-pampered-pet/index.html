A small family salon since 2002

The salon opened in 2002 and is still run by one family. Reviewers describe the owner, his wife and their sons working the shop together, and regulars ask for Oscar by name. The pace is unhurried, and nervous dogs and cats get the time they need.

Questions owners ask first

If yours is not here, call or put it in the request form.

Do you groom cats?

Yes. In the salon's own words: while some pet groomers in Millbrae only groom dogs, The Pampered Pet loves your feline friends, too. Baths, trims, nails and ears.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your pet's size and coat and on what you choose from the service list. Call (650) 952-7600 with the breed, size and what you need and you will get a quote.

Do you do teeth?

Tooth brushing with a specialty toothpaste and a flexible brush is on the service list. Ask when you call if you want it added to a groom.

When are you open?

Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Closed Sunday. The salon is at 111 Park Place in Millbrae, just off Broadway.

When a door stops, we come out.

Most calls are one of the problems below. Tell us what you hear and see, and we bring the right parts on the first trip.

What you noticeWhat we do
A loud bang, then the door will not liftAlmost always a broken spring. We replace torsion and extension springs, plus the cables, drums and pulleys that go with them.
The opener runs but the door does not moveWe repair Chamberlain, Sears, LiftMaster and Stanley openers, and when one is worn out we install a new LiftMaster or Genie.
The door hangs crooked or came off the trackCables, drums and rollers. We get the door back on its track, balanced and safe to use.
The handle spins or the door will not lockLatches, locks, T-handles and release hardware, repaired or replaced.
The remote stopped workingGenie Intellicode and LiftMaster Security+ remotes, reprogrammed or replaced.
Nothing is broken yetRegular maintenance keeps it that way. We check the springs, lubricate the moving parts and look at anything wearing out.

Sixty years on the Peninsula

AL-Tone was founded by Tony & Lucy Carapiet in Daly City in 1964 and has been in continuous operation since then. Al-Tone was later purchased in 1989 by Edgar Velasquez & Erasmo Martinez.

We pride ourselves in prompt services, responsible attendance, fair prices and friendly conduct. We specialize in residential installation, service and repair, and we sell, install and service Wayne Dalton wood and metal sectional doors and most LiftMaster and Genie openers across San Francisco and San Mateo counties.

Our story

From an MGM starlet to a third generation of dancers

Georgia's School of Dance is the oldest dance studio in North County. Founder Georgia "Georgie" Copeland began her professional career at eight, was chosen as an MGM Starlet at seventeen, and worked with Fred Astaire, Lucille Ball, Buddy Ebsen and Esther Williams before opening her studio in Escondido in 1953. Through her instruction, she personally produced over 40 professional dancers.

After Georgia's passing in 1998, Sue Gilson, who had taught alongside Georgia for more than 25 years, took over the studio and has continued her legacy of excellent dance technique and real opportunities for dancers in North San Diego County. Sue is also an Associate Professor of Dance at Palomar College and a member of the Escondido Chamber of Commerce.

The studio has called 142 E. Grand Ave home for decades and now spans four dance floors after expanding into the building next door. Sue's motto says it best: "Keep Dancing."

Recital

The 73rd Annual Recital: "Strike a Pose"

Every June the whole school takes the stage at the California Center for the Arts, Escondido, for a full production with more than 40 class numbers across three shows. The 73rd Annual Recital filled three performances on June 26 and 27, 2026, with tickets at the front desk ($25 adults, $14 children).

Recital video and next year's dates are announced at the studio and on Instagram.

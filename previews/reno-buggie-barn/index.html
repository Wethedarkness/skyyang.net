A Reno VW shop since 1972

Reno Buggie Barn has been a family owned and operated business since 1972, making it one of the oldest Volkswagen repair shops in the Reno area. We specialize in both air-cooled and water-cooled Volkswagen cars, everything from your '56 oval window to your 2013 Jetta.

You'll find Spencer at the front desk. Customers who have brought their Bugs, buses, Ghias and 914s here for decades say the same things: the work is first class, and the shop explains what it did and why.

We started on East 2nd Street and are now at 295 Gentry Way, Suite 12, with over 70 years of combined experience in the shop and a machine shop in-house.

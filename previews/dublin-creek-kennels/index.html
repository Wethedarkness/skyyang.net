About us

Nestled in a wooded glen in Dublin's historic district

Dublin Creek Kennels is family owned and operated by Luke and Tammy Seidlitz, who each bring more than 35 years of professional experience in the care, handling and showing of dogs across all breeds. They are lifelong cat owners too.

We think of ourselves as going beyond the traditional kenneling operation: someone is on the property around the clock, our staff learn every pet's name and quirks, and special diets and medical needs are simply part of the routine.

We take the time to give your pet the love and attention they require in your absence. Tours are welcome during business hours; just leave your pet at home for the visit.

Before you check in

What to bring

Please bring proof of current vaccinations from your veterinarian at check-in. Pets that have been exposed to rabies, distemper, parvo, bordetella or an upper-respiratory illness in the last 30 days cannot be boarded.

  • Dogs: Rabies, DHPP and Bordetella (dog flu vaccine recommended)
  • Cats: Rabies, FVRCP and Feline Leukemia (or a negative test within 90 days)
  • Washable bedding only; food, treats and litter are provided
  • Your vet's contact information and an emergency contact
  • Reservation requests are confirmed within 24 hours

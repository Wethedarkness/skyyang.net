Process

Four steps, no surprises

Inquiry

Send styles, target quantities, target price and your delivery date. A tech pack helps; a reference photo is enough to start.

Sample

We develop the fit and wash, send the sample, and revise until you approve it in hand.

Production

Bulk cutting, sewing, washing and finishing to the approved sample, with in-line and final inspection.

Shipping

Export packing, documents and freight to your warehouse or 3PL, by sea or air.

About

Made in the town that makes jeans

Xintang, in Guangzhou's Zengcheng district, is known as the world's denim capital: fabric mills, cut-and-sew workshops, wash houses and trim suppliers sit within a few kilometers of each other, which is why fit changes and wash revisions happen in days here rather than weeks.

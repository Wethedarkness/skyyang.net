How a full day goes

Parents ask for this more than anything else, so it sits on the page. Drop off, ski, eat, ski, pick up with photos. Times shift a little with the weather and the group.

  1. 8:30Drop-offBoot and helmet check, name tag on the bib, a quick word about your kid's day.
  2. 9:00Warm-upGames on the magic carpet, then the group finds its level for the day.
  3. 10:30Hot chocolateTwenty minutes inside, mittens on the dryer.
  4. 11:00Second blockFirst chairlift for the bigger kids, more carpet laps for the small ones.
  5. 12:15LunchWarm lunch at the base, allergies handled from your booking notes.
  6. 13:15Afternoon runsThe part of the day where it clicks. Coaches film a short clip for you.
  7. 14:45Wind-downStretch, stickers, gear back in the bag.
  8. 15:15PickupTwo-minute recap from the coach, the day's photos sent to your phone.

Good to know before you land

Bring

  • Helmet and goggles (we can rent both)
  • Mittens, not gloves, and a spare pair
  • Neck warmer instead of a scarf
  • Sunscreen, even on grey days
  • A small snack they actually like

We handle

  • Skis, boots and poles sized on day one
  • Lift tickets for lesson hours
  • Lunch and hot chocolate on full days
  • The tracker bib and a name tag
  • Shuttle from Hirafu accommodation on request

Questions parents ask

Short answers here, the long version in your booking confirmation.

My kid has never seen snow. Is a full day too much?

Usually yes for a first day under six. Start with First skis or a half day, then move to full days once they ask for more. We will tell you honestly at pickup.

Can two kids of different ages be in the same group?

Groups are by ability, not age, so a seven-year-old and a nine-year-old who are both starting out will often ski together. Under-fives stay in First skis.

What happens on a very cold or windy day?

We message you by 7 am. Lessons stay low on the carpet with more indoor breaks, or switch to snow play, or we credit the day. You are never charged for a day we cancel.

Do you speak English?

All coaches teach in English. Several also speak Japanese, and we can arrange Mandarin or Cantonese for private lessons with notice.

How far ahead should we book?

For late December to mid February, six to eight weeks. Shoulder weeks often have space a few days out. Check your dates above and we will tell you the same day.

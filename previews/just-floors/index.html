A Concord flooring store since 1976

Just Floors was established in 1976. Our building at 1051 Detroit Avenue was constructed for Just Floors, and the showroom and warehouse are stocked with carpet, laminate, hardwood and tile.

Owner Chris Hopfner has been in the flooring business since 1988, with hands-on experience as an installer in wood, tile, carpet and laminate. The family also owns Pacific Coast Carpet in Concord and Lamorinda Floors in Lafayette, and the buying power of three stores keeps prices, quality and selection strong.

Our professional salespeople will help with every phase of your project, and our installation teams are certified to install everything we sell. Professional estimates are scheduled daily in your home.

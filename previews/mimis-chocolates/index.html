From a farmers-market table to a Dublin storefront

Mimi's Chocolates began in 2016 when Chef Mia D. Jenkins, a Bay Area native and Johnson & Wales culinary graduate, started selling her caramel confections on Instagram, at pop-ups and at the San Ramon farmers market.

Her mother Natalie joined as co-owner in 2018, the kitchen moved to Berkeley, and in 2022 the family opened its own storefront on Dublin Boulevard.

We specialize in handcrafted confections, rooted in tradition but elevated with detail, soul and grace. And we don't have customers, we have friends and family.

About us

Twenty-six years on Main Street

Fusion 3 Salon opened in downtown Pleasanton in February 2000 and has been serving guests from Pleasanton and the surrounding area ever since. Owner Larry came to hairdressing when his wife was studying to become a hairdresser: he went along for orientation and decided he wanted to study it too.

We are an Aveda concept salon that also carries Davines. Unlike commission and rental salons, we're not solely focused on top-line sales. We welcome and encourage you to see multiple stylists, rescheduling is never a problem because our entire team is available to you, and every one of our stylists has completed our in-house training program.

Our promise is simple: if you don't receive the best salon service you've ever had, we'll refund your money with no questions asked.

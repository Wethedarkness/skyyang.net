Family-owned in Pleasanton for 44 years

Valley Plumbing Home Center was established in 1982 and has grown from a family-owned business into a corporation with the same family feeling. In 2005 we moved to our downtown home on Rose Avenue, one block off Main Street, under one of only six old-time neon signs left in downtown Pleasanton.

Owners Mike and Sharol Cheney both grew up in Pleasanton, live here and raised their family here. Our motto, “Integrity Before Profit,” comes straight from our mission: superior service through the highest standards of quality, reliability and dependability.

Our technicians arrive in marked vans, text ahead with their name and arrival time, and explain the options before any work starts. No hard sell, just honest expert advice.

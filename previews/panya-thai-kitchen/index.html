Menu

From the Panya kitchen

A sample of what we cook every day. Curries, noodles and rice dishes are served family style with your choice of mixed vegetables or tofu, mock duck, chicken, pork, beef, roasted duck, BBQ pork, shrimp, calamari or fish.

Starters & salads

  • Thai Spring Rolls

    Stuffed with seasoned cabbage, silver noodles and black mushrooms. Golden fried and served with plum sauce.

  • Sa-Tay

    Chicken, pork or tofu marinated in coconut milk and yellow curry spices, skewered and pan-grilled. Served with peanut sauce and cucumber relish.

  • Kanom Jeeb

    Steamed Thai dumplings stuffed with seasoned ground shrimp and chicken. Served with sweet garlic soy sauce.

  • Larb

    Ground chicken or beef tossed with lime juice, fish sauce, rice powder, onions and fresh mint. Served with a wedge of cabbage.

  • Som Tum

    Shredded green papaya, long beans, tomatoes, peanuts, Thai chilies, lime juice and palm sugar, crushed with a mortar and pestle. Vegetarian, shrimp or black crab.

  • Panya Chicken Salad

    Chopped salad with seasoned grilled chicken, cucumbers, tomatoes and crispy noodles with Panya's horseradish dressing.

Noodles, rice & curries

Family style, priced by your choice of protein.

  • Phad Thai

    Thin rice noodles wok-fried with egg, tamarind juice, bean sprouts, green onions and peanuts.

  • Spicy Drunken Noodles

    Wide rice noodles wok-fried with soy, ground chili, basil, bean sprouts, onions and bell peppers.

  • Grapow Fried Rice

    Wok-fried with soy, ground chili, garlic, basil and bell peppers.

  • Pineapple Fried Rice

    Wok-fried with pineapple, cashews, green onions and pepper.

  • Green Curry

    Bamboo shoots, eggplant, basil and bell pepper.

  • Panang Curry

    Sweet and spicy with red bell peppers and basil.

  • Massaman Curry

    With potato, onion, carrot and bell pepper.

  • Cashew Nuts

    Cashew nuts, dried chili, carrots, onions and green onions.

Soups, noodle soups & specialties

  • Tom Yum Soup

    Hot and sour lemongrass soup with roasted chili, lemon leaves, fresh mushrooms and green onions. Bowl or pot.

  • Thai Boat Noodles

    Seasoned stew broth, braised pork, pork meatballs, bean sprouts and green onions. Choose wide rice, thin rice or egg noodles.

  • Khag Curry Noodles

    Yellow curry broth, stewed chicken, bean sprouts, toasted onion and a hard-boiled egg.

  • Thai Style Ribs

    Pork spare ribs marinated in garlic and pepper soy sauce, served on a bed of cabbage on a sizzle plate.

  • Prince's Duck

    Half roasted duck with honeyed ginger and black bean sauce, served on a bed of steamed broccoli.

  • Orange Chicken

    Thai style orange chicken prepared with fresh oranges.

About us

Family-owned in Escondido since 2006

Panya Thai Kitchen has been a family-owned restaurant on West Valley Parkway since 2006, just off I-15 with a free parking lot out front.

The kitchen is open to the dining room, so you can hear the wok going and smell the garlic and basil while you wait. Dishes come out fast, and every one of them is made to your spice level, from 1 to 10.

Come in for the daily lunch special, bring the family for dinner, sit outside when the weather is nice, or order online and pick it up on the way home.

Lunch specials

A plate lunch every day, 11 to 3

Lunch specials are served plate-lunch style: your main dish with soup, salad, fried spring rolls, fried wontons and steamed rice (noodle and fried-rice dishes come on their own). Pick a dish, pick a protein, pick your heat.

  • Garlic: in garlic and pepper sauce on a bed of cabbage
  • Green Chili (Sweet Basil): Thai basil, bell peppers, green chili, onions, garlic, carrots and mushrooms
  • Panang Curry: light, sweet and spicy with fresh basil and bell pepper
  • Massaman Curry: potato, onion, carrot and bell pepper
  • Phad Thai Noodles: wok-fried rice noodles with egg, bean sprouts, green onions and crushed peanuts
  • Spicy Drunken Noodles: wide rice noodles with chili, garlic, bean sprouts, bell pepper and basil

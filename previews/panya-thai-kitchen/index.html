Starters and salads

Thai Spring Rolls

Stuffed with seasoned cabbage, silver noodles and black mushrooms. Golden fried and served with plum sauce.

Sa-Tay

Chicken, pork or tofu marinated in coconut milk and yellow curry spices, skewered and pan-grilled. Served with peanut sauce and cucumber relish.

Kanom Jeeb

Steamed Thai dumplings stuffed with seasoned ground shrimp and chicken. Served with sweet garlic soy sauce.

Larb

Ground chicken or beef tossed with lime juice, fish sauce, rice powder, onions and fresh mint. Served with a wedge of cabbage.

Som Tum

Shredded green papaya, long beans, tomatoes, peanuts, Thai chilies, lime juice and palm sugar, crushed with a mortar and pestle. Vegetarian, shrimp or black crab.

Panya Chicken Salad

Chopped salad with seasoned grilled chicken, cucumbers, tomatoes and crispy noodles with Panya's horseradish dressing.

Family-owned in Escondido since 2006

Panya Thai Kitchen has been a family-owned restaurant on West Valley Parkway since 2006, just off I-15 with a free parking lot out front.

The kitchen is open to the dining room, so you can hear the wok going and smell the garlic and basil while you wait. Dishes come out fast, and every one of them is made to your spice level, from 1 to 10.

Come in for the daily lunch special, bring the family for dinner, sit outside when the weather is nice, or order online and pick it up on the way home.

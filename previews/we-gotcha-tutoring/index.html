What sessions look like

From day one to the long-term shift, here is how it plays out.

The first session

The focus is not homework. It is about getting to know one another and figuring out where to start. We talk about the student's goals, explain what to expect, and do a quick practice assessment to see what they know, where they are struggling, and what to work on together.

The rest

Regular 50 to 60 minute sessions online, built around the student's current classes. The schoolwork is the material; the skill of learning is the lesson.

Why I built We Gotcha

Students were fine learning with guidance, but the second they were asked to learn something on their own, they froze.

Growing up, I didn't have much. Coming from a family of generations of factory workers, I assumed I'd do that as well. Education changed my outlook: it gave me a way out and a lot more to be excited about. As a tutor, I want that for my students too, a real shot at finding out what they're capable of.

Teaching myself to draw showed me the missing piece. The artists I learned from weren't special because of talent. Their strongest skill was learning itself. Once I studied how learning works, my drawing improved faster than it ever had, and I knew I had to share it with my students. If I can teach them how to learn, I can help with their classes now and with everything after.

Tyler, founder. MS in Data Science, BS in Math and Philosophy, curriculum designer and tutor trainer.

Questions parents ask first

If yours is not here, put it in the request form and it gets answered before any session is scheduled.

Is everything online?

Yes. Every session is online and runs 50 to 60 minutes, whether it is 1-on-1, a small group or learning-coach work.

What happens in the first session?

No homework. We get to know each other, talk about the student's goals, explain what to expect, and do a quick practice assessment to see what they know and where they are stuck. That tells us where to start.

How does the tiered pricing work?

You pick the tier that reflects your circumstances: $75 standard, $40 reduced, or a flexible rate if even that is out of reach. No paperwork and no income verification. Group sessions are $20 less at every tier.

What ages and subjects?

Elementary school through college, plus adults. Math, English, science, history and test prep, with other subjects on request.

Who is the tutor?

Tyler, the founder: more than ten years of tutoring, 500 or more students, an MS in Data Science and a BS in Math and Philosophy, and experience designing curriculum and training other tutors.

Ready? Let's figure it out.

Send a few lines about what is going on and you will hear back by email. The first session is where we work out the plan.

How these were built

  • Companies from Google Maps place data for each metro, deduplicated by website domain.
  • Decision-maker names read from each company's own About or Team page.
  • Emails found with an enrichment waterfall and kept only when they resolve on the company's own domain and pass a bounce check.
  • Phones are the company main lines from Google Maps. Blanks mean nothing reliable was found; nothing is guessed.
  • L04 to L06: companies sized with LinkedIn company records, people taken from LinkedIn company rosters (titles as listed there), direct phones from the same enrichment providers.

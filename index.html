I'm building Imagine AI (YC F25), based in San Francisco. We're a research and software company trying to model modern B2B growth. The question I'm stuck on is how trust moves between people and companies, and what AI is quietly doing to it.

I come at this as a practitioner more than a theorist. Most of what I believe comes from sitting inside real companies and watching what actually earns attention, one experiment at a time. The topics I keep coming back to are

  • game theory,
  • network economics,
  • resource allocation,
  • and econometrics.

Before this, my training was in political economy rather than software: UC San Diego (where I was student body president), a short research stint at Cambridge, and Break the Outbreak, a nonprofit I ran through the pandemic. Those years taught me more about how people organize than any theory did.

I learn by building things, putting them in front of people, and writing down what happens. I'd call myself an empiricist more than a visionary; I trust what I can test.
